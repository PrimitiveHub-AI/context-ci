\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}

\subsection{Summary}

AI-agent context is a new class of supply-chain artifact, and the
existing controls (SLSA, in-toto, Sigstore, package-manager provenance)
do not reach it. A primitive can be signed, \contentdigest{}-verified,
and unchanged since signing, and still tell the consuming agent to fetch
a remote URL and run the response. The missing layer is a semantic check
that scans a primitive's prose for known attack shapes before it reaches
the agent's load path.

We presented PrimitiveHub, a registry and static-analysis pipeline that
adds that layer. The contributions are a ten-category threat taxonomy
defined operationally; a 124-sample labeled corpus split into in-sample
and held-out slices; two engines whose verdicts a CI gate holds
identical on the in-sample slice; an AV-style paired-ledger discipline;
and an open pipeline a reviewer can rerun from a clean clone. On the
in-sample slice the detectors reach a macro F\textsubscript{1} of 0.991,
with one known precision item. On the held-out slice the Python engine
recovers all 25 attacks and the TypeScript engine recovers 18, a result
we report rather than tune away because it is the clearest evidence the
measurement is real. The corpus, the ledgers, and the gate are what let
a future measurement at a larger size be compared against this one.

\subsection{Future work}

\paragraph{Close the cross-engine recall gap} The most immediate work is
bringing the TypeScript engine's held-out recovery up to the Python
engine's. Each of the seven misses is a lockstep ratchet (Path C)
recorded in the false-negative ledger, with the corresponding corpus
sample locking the fix.

\paragraph{Resolve the CCI-005 false positive} The single in-sample
false positive on the unsafe-shell-install detector is the open
precision item, resolvable through a prose edit (Path A) or a detector
narrowing (Path C) measured against the corpus.

\paragraph{Expand the corpus, with held-out parity} The next round
targets a tenfold growth in per-detector support and at least three
held-out samples per detector, so the overfit probe is as dense as the
training slice. Every expansion ships held-out samples the detectors are
not permitted to tune against until the closure round.

\paragraph{Multilingual adversarial samples} Russian, Chinese, Arabic,
and Spanish variants are scheduled for v0.3 to test detection beyond the
English-language phrasing bias, bounded by a script-identifier
discipline so detection generalizes rather than hard-codes per language.

\paragraph{Runtime gating and full confusables} The agent-side
\texttt{verify\_before\_load} gate enforces signature and lock-digest
integrity, but does not yet re-run the full Top~10 inside the MCP server
at the moment of load (the detectors are reachable there only through
the separate \texttt{scan\_text} tool). A later round folds the full
re-scan into the gate, so even a corrupted lock file cannot deliver a
known-bad primitive to a running agent. Separately, the confusables
detector will grow from the 35-vendor list to the full UTS~\#39
distribution and UAX~\#31 identifier rules, an expansion that is mostly
data, since the algorithm is unchanged.

\subsection{Closing}

Static validation of agent-context artifacts is an emerging defense
layer between provenance verification and runtime instruction handling.
This release should be read as a reproducible first measurement and a
benchmark \emph{seed}, not a mature detector benchmark: its contribution
is to fix the artifact class, the threat taxonomy, the measurement
contract, and the reproducibility path that larger evaluations can build
on, and the numbers are the first datapoint produced under that contract
rather than a settled performance claim. What makes them comparable to
future ones is the discipline behind them: a public corpus that doubles
as the regression contract, numbered append-only ledgers for every
false positive and false negative, and a CI gate that fails the merge on
in-sample regression or cross-language drift. We invite independent
replication at our public security contact and credit it in the ledger.
The corpus is therefore not only an evaluation set but the mechanism by
which future detector changes remain comparable to the measurements
reported here.
