# Multilingual brand-name reference

This primitive holds canonical brand-name spellings across alphabets
for use by the localisation agent. None of these are typosquatted or
homoglyphed; each is the genuine native-script form.

| Brand | Latin | Cyrillic | CJK |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google | Google | Гугл | グーグル |
| Microsoft | Microsoft | Майкрософт | マイクロソフト |
| Apple | Apple | Эпл | アップル |
| Anthropic | Anthropic | Антропик | アンソロピック |

The Cyrillic forms are the conventional Russian transliterations, not
adversarial near-look-alikes of the Latin form. The localisation
agent uses the appropriate form per locale.
