# Canonical-docs index

The project ships three measurement artifacts alongside the wire-format
specs:

- Trust Contract v0.1 (specification — ships under MIT for the
  reference implementation).
- Context CI Top 10 v0.1 (specification — ships under MIT for the
  textual content).
- Adversarial Corpus v0.1 (labeled dataset — ships under CC-BY-4.0
  (corpus content) to permit academic citation).

The corpus license CC-BY-4.0 (corpus content) is intentionally
distinct from the codebase license (MIT for measurement scripts).
This primitive describes what license each artifact ships under;
no content is borrowed from external CC-BY sources.
