# Citation-grounding primitive

This primitive describes how to ground an agent response in
verifiable scholarly references. Two reference forms are common:

- Crossref DOIs (most journal articles), e.g.
  DOI: 10.1109/ICSE.2020.00094
- arXiv preprints, citable either via `arXiv:2401.12345` or via the
  Crossref DOI form, e.g. DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2401.12345

Both forms are resolvable via doi.org and arxiv.org respectively.
The 10.48550 prefix is the registered arXiv DOI authority.
