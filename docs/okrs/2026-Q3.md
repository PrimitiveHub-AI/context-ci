## Objective 1 — Establish PrimitiveHub as the standards-setting body for AI-agent context security.

**Why this objective:** Operating Laws 1–4 and our category position
depend on owning the vocabulary. Whoever names the attacks owns the
category (OWASP Top 10 precedent). Standards-setting work is cheap and
compounds.

| KR | Target | Gate | Status |
|----|--------|------|--------|
| 1.1 | **Context CI Top 10 v0.1** spec published with stable IDs CCI-001 … CCI-010, named attacks, severity scoring, examples drawn from the corpus, prevention guidance. | G-A (Week 2) | ◯ planned |
| 1.2 | **Trust Contract v0.1 conformance suite** live: ≥30 golden Trust Bundles + ≥10 adversarial ones, all green against the live HTTP API. | G-A (Week 2) | ◯ planned |
| 1.3 | **CBOM v0.1 spec** published and submitted to at least one standards body (SPDX, CycloneDX, or IETF). Acknowledgment counts as success. | G-F (Week 16) | ◯ planned |

## Objective 2 — Ship the Trust Runtime kernel that competitors and embedders adopt.

**Why this objective:** Operating Laws 5, 7, 9. The verifier embedded
by other tools is what turns PrimitiveHub from a product into
infrastructure. ICP-3 (model providers / IDE vendors) is the embed
audience.

| KR | Target | Gate | Status |
|----|--------|------|--------|
| 2.1 | **`@primitivehub/verify` v0.1** published to npm under MIT. < 50KB minified, zero runtime deps in core. 100% conformance suite pass. | G-B (Week 4) | ◯ planned |
| 2.2 | **`phub` CLI v0.1** + **Context CI GitHub Action v1** both published. End-to-end demo: a PR introducing a Top 10 finding fails CI; SARIF appears in GitHub's security tab. | G-C (Week 7) | ◯ planned |
| 2.3 | At least **one external embed** of `@primitivehub/verify` live in an OSS project (informal integrations count). | G-F (Week 16) | ◯ planned |

## Objective 3 — Make the paper claim provable at launch.

**Why this objective:** Operating Laws 6 and 7. The paper is the
credibility moat. The paper's claim — "Context CI detects these
attacks reproducibly at this precision/recall" — must be provable on
demand to an academic reviewer or a hostile journalist.

| KR | Target | Gate | Status |
|----|--------|------|--------|
| 3.1 | **5 Top 10 detectors** with precision/recall numbers on a labeled corpus of ≥50 known-bad + ≥50 known-good + ≥20 ambiguous primitives. Reproducible via documented eval harness. | G-D (Week 9) | ◯ planned |
| 3.2 | **3 design partners** signed (NDA) and actively using the Context CI GitHub Action in their own CI. Mix of GCC enterprise + US/EU mid-market + OSS community. | G-F (Week 16) | ◯ planned |
| 3.3 | **Academic co-author** signed on the paper. CMU CyLab, ETH SecLab, KAUST, or equivalent. Co-author letter signed. | G-F (Week 14) | ◯ planned |

## Tracking cadence

- **Weekly (Mon):** Update KR status icons. Any KR slipping ≥3 days
  triggers an explicit "should we cut scope or move the gate?"
  decision documented in the weekly notes.
- **Monthly (last Fri):** Walk all 9 KRs in a retro. Update
  [`bugs.md`](../bugs.md) with anything that surprised us.
- **End of quarter (2026-09-30):** Mark each KR as hit / partial /
  miss. Write a short retro at `docs/okrs/2026-Q3-retro.md`. Use the
  learnings to set 2026-Q4 OKRs.

## What this OKR set does NOT track

Discipline is what we cut. The following are deliberately off this OKR
set:

- **GitHub stars** — vanity metric per doctrine.
- **Registered users** — vanity metric per doctrine.
- **Number of primitives published** — vanity metric per doctrine.
- **Hiring** — necessary but tracked elsewhere (R-3 in
  [`../execution-plan.md`](../execution-plan.md) risk register).
- **Federation / self-hosted Sigstore / marketplace search** —
  explicitly deferred per `execution-plan.md` § "What does NOT ship."
- **Pricing tier rollout** — design-partner contracts are bespoke
  until Q4. Self-serve pricing is not a Q3 KR.
