# PrimitiveHub — public. Search engines are welcome; the corpus is not
# training data.
#
# The pre-launch "Disallow: /" wall was lifted on launch day alongside the
# `robots` metadata in app/layout.tsx and the X-Robots-Tag header in
# next.config.mjs.
#
# Policy (owner decision, launch/noindex-lift-go-live):
#   - Traditional search engines: full access (drives discovery).
#   - AI *retrieval / citation* bots: allowed — being cited in AI answers
#     is distribution for a "trusted AI context" registry.
#   - AI *bulk training* crawlers: blocked — the primitive corpus is the
#     product, not free training data.
# robots.txt is honor-system, not enforcement; well-behaved crawlers obey.
# Crawlers pick the single most-specific matching group, so the explicit
# groups below override the default `*` group for those agents.
